# Self-Drive Rental Rules & Guidelines

1. **Driver Age Requirement**: Minimum age of 21 years with at least 1 year of holding a valid driver's license.
2. **Identification**: Valid Government Photo ID (Passport or Driver's License) required at vehicle pickup.
3. **Speed Limits**: Vehicles are equipped with speed tracking devices. Adhere strictly to national highway speed regulations.
4. **Fuel Policy**: Vehicles are provided with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank.
