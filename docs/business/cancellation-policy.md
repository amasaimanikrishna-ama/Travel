# Cancellation Policy

- **Full Refund (100%)**: Cancellations made 48 hours or more before the scheduled trip or rental pickup time.
- **Partial Refund (50%)**: Cancellations made between 24 and 48 hours before pickup.
- **Non-Refundable (0%)**: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup or in the event of a no-show.
